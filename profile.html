❓ Help Center & Knowledge Base

Find quick answers to common questions about Language Lab

Complete all required lessons in your active realm with at least 1 star. Once finished, the next realm on the world map will automatically unlock for you!

When prompted during speaking activities, click the microphone button and allow browser microphone permissions. Speak clearly into your mic, and the engine will grade your pronunciation in real-time.

Stars reflect your overall score in each module. Getting 80%+ accuracy awards 2 stars, and 95%+ awards all 3 gold stars. Accuracy rate tracks correct answers across all completed quizzes.

Click the "🎨 Change Avatar" button at the top of your Profile page. Switch between Boy and Girl avatar collections, select your favorite, and click "Select & Save Avatar".

Under Profile Settings > Notification Preferences, check or uncheck assignment reminders, lesson alerts, and announcements. You can also view all notifications by clicking "View All Notifications".
